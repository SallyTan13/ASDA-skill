## Pattern: CAPM Mispricing Nuanced Interpretation

**Description:** Correctly interpreting slight differences between CAPM-calculated and actual expected returns requires careful analysis beyond mechanical calculation.

**When to Use:** When evaluating stock pricing using Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), especially with marginal return differences

**Procedure:**
1. Calculate CAPM expected return using formula: E(Ri) = Rf + βi[E(Rm) - Rf]
2. Compare calculated return to actual expected return
3. Assess magnitude of deviation, not just algebraic difference
4. Interpret deviation context:
   - Tiny differences (< 0.5%) likely indicate market efficiency
   - Larger deviations (> 1%) suggest potential mispricing
5. Verify interpretation by checking:
   - Consistency with market conditions
   - Potential transaction costs of arbitrage
   - Overall market efficiency assumptions

**Example:**
> **Scenario:** A stock has CAPM return of 10.2% vs. actual return of 10%
> **Wrong approach:** Immediately conclude stock is underpriced
> **Correct approach:** Recognize 0.2% difference is negligible, likely within market pricing noise
